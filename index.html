    What botanicals are part of the KeshVeda story?

    Shikakai, reetha, amla, bhringraj, hibiscus, curry leaves, fenugreek seeds, neem leaves, pomegranate peel, flaxseed, and aloe vera. See the product packaging for the complete ingredient list.

    How should I use the shampoo?

    Wet your hair, gently lather a small amount through your scalp and hair, then rinse thoroughly. Repeat if needed, and follow any instructions on the product label.

    How should I store the bottle?

    Keep the bottle closed and store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Always follow the storage guidance printed on the product packaging.

    How can I order, and what about delivery?

    Choose 150 mL for ₹249, 250 mL for ₹349, or 500 mL for ₹599 on the product page. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 87937 42912 to confirm availability, delivery options, and any applicable charges before ordering.

    Is it suitable for every scalp and hair type?

    Everyone’s hair and skin are different. Review the packaging for ingredients, consider a small patch test before first use, and stop using the product if irritation occurs.

    Does it prevent hair fall or promise hair growth?

    No. KeshVeda is an everyday cosmetic shampoo and does not promise hair growth, prevent hair fall, or treat a medical condition.
